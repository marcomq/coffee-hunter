# Generated art provenance

All game art was generated with the built-in image generation tool. The original Java assets were visual references only and are not consumed by the Godot project.

The approved direction is original SNES-era 16-bit pixel art: a stern coffee adventurer, cheerful and dim tea creatures, appetizing roasted beans, used coffee-filter hazards, calm top-down terrain, and soft vertical shadows. `style-guide.png` records the approved tropical/golden/coral palette exploration.

Final generation prompts required:

- Hero: four-direction transparent sprite sheet; brown hat, red neckerchief, net, backpack; identical proportions and palette.
- Tea filter: clean pale-mint damp paper, golden lower stain, berry tag, happily vacant face; transparent background.
- Coffee beans: five compact elongated roasted beans; no steam, aroma, leaves, dirt or sparkles; transparent background.
- Coffee filter: pale fluted paper filled with fine wet spent grounds; no whole beans; transparent background.
- Teapot: clean turquoise-and-cream ceramic, silly face, exact uppercase `TEA`; transparent background.
- Tropical tiles: quiet ground and dug tunnel pair, broad color areas, sparse texture, vertical wall shadow, no characters or decoration.
- Tropical level background: one continuous calm coffee-jungle scene spanning the full board, sparse asymmetric detail, broad playable center and no tile boundaries, characters, tunnels or UI.
